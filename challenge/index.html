The Maplewood challenge

  1. Choose the same challenge

    Start chapter 1 fresh. Choose Explorer difficulty and solo mode. For a family team challenge, compare co-op scores separately.

  2. Play for skill

    Defeat scouts, collect gear, and land charged counters. A clear earns a bonus. Missed landings and life losses reduce the score.

  3. Share your moment

    After the chapter, choose Share score or Save image. Invite a friend to try the same chapter, difficulty, and mode.

Your top scores stay on your device. This is a friendly self-reported challenge. There is no public ranking, prize, or score submission form.
