Controls

Two players: Player 2 uses A / D to move, W to jump, F to fire, and E to dash. Player 1 uses ← / → to move, ↑ to jump, Enter to fire, and right Shift to dash. With Ninja Nugs, release and press jump again in midair. Kairo’s dash becomes a screen-wide tsunami after 10 seconds without taking a hit. Nugs’s dash throws a screen-wide smoke bomb after 10 seconds without taking a hit. Cash’s dash calls lightning after 10 seconds without either player getting hit.
